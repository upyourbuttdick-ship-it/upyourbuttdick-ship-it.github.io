Dick's Autobaiter

Dick's Autobaiter is a privately operated desktop application designed to assist with identifying, documenting, and responding to suspected email scams.

How It Works

The application connects to an authorized Gmail account to retrieve messages and send responses. It is operated locally by its owner and is not offered as a public service.

Privacy

The application processes email information for the owner's personal anti-fraud activities. It does not sell personal information or provide public access to Gmail data.
