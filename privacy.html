Privacy Policy — Dick's Autobaiter

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Overview

Dick's Autobaiter is a privately operated desktop application used for personal anti-fraud activities. It is not offered as a public service.

Google Gmail Data

The application accesses an authorized Gmail account to read email messages and send responses.

Data Storage

Email information and application records are stored locally on the owner's computer. The application does not operate a public database or cloud storage service.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell Gmail data or share it with advertisers. Email responses are transmitted through Gmail to their intended recipients.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored records are retained for the owner's anti-fraud activities and may be deleted by the owner.

Security

Access to Gmail is authorized through Google's OAuth authentication system.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to upyourbuttdick@gmail.com.
